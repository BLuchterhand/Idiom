Allowed veto right values: yes / no.

## Fit Check

The map is complete only when every named owner has the control needed for the
responsibility they carry.

If a person owns the outcome but lacks source access, review time, authority to
reject, escalation cover, or budget, the workflow has accountability without
authority. Do not increase authority until the repair is named.

## Common Repairs

| Gap | Repair |
| --- | --- |
| Reviewer owns quality but lacks time. | Fund reviewer-hours or throttle packets. |
| Support owner carries customer outcome but finance owns approval. | Split packet preparation from financial acceptance. |
| Source owner maintains records but cannot resolve business exceptions. | Add escalation owner and source-conflict state. |
| Executive sponsor wants speed but does not own risk. | Require stop/go decision and accepted residual risk. |
| Governance owns policy but not runtime behavior. | Convert policy into packet fields, permissions, logs, and stop conditions. |
