## Production Record

The EPUB exporter treats `cover.jpg` as the required release cover and fails if
it is missing. `cover.svg` is kept as the editable artwork source and is copied
to `exports/cover.svg` for inspection. For this beta/proof package, `cover.jpg`
is the approved rasterization of `cover.svg`; the exported JPG is byte-identical
to that source JPG.

The workspace does not contain evidence that any cover element was generated by
AI. It also does not contain the original rasterization command or tool log that
created `cover.jpg` from `cover.svg`. The final KDP AI-content disclosure answer
therefore remains an external launch gate: before upload, confirm the author's
cover-production record and record the disclosure answer in the KDP workflow.
