# Source Ledger

Purpose: track sources that have been reviewed closely enough to cite in
publication-facing draft prose.

Relationship to other files:

- `research-sources.md`: reusable source pool and source IDs.
- `compact-source-crosswalk.md`: chapter-level source routing and claim gates.
- `source-gap-backlog.md`: missing evidence to research before strong claims.
- `source-ledger.md`: citation-ready source notes after a source has been read
  and checked.

Do not cite a source in polished prose only because it appears in
`research-sources.md`. Move it here after review.

## Citation Status

Chapter prose now uses author-date citations for both case sources and
research/governance sources. Bracket IDs such as `RC1`, `A2`, or `G2` remain
ledger handles only. They should not appear in publication-facing chapter prose.

Chicago author-date is the selected publication style. Before final production,
verify citation consistency and links.
