# Trust the Process, Not the Prompt

## How to Turn AI Output Into Trusted Work

AI output starts as a proposal. A workflow accepts it for use only after
required evidence is checked and a named owner accepts the decision or
authorizes the permitted effect.

This practical field guide is for leaders and senior practitioners moving
generative AI from experiments into real workflows. It shows how to design one
AI workflow where useful model output becomes accepted work only through
evidence, source freshness, permissions, review capacity, accountable
acceptance, and learning.

## What The Book Helps You Do

- Decide whether a workflow should use AI for discovery only, draft assistance,
  reviewed proposal, or bounded action.
- Map AI-generated claims to authority-bearing sources.
- Define what AI may access, communicate, mutate, trigger, retry, or escalate.
- Measure accepted verified output instead of generated volume.
- Build reviewer packets that give reviewers evidence, time, and authority to
  reject.
- Detect shadow AI as a workflow-pressure signal.
- Run a narrow pilot that can continue, narrow, throttle, escalate, or stop.
