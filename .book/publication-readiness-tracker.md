Status key:

- `Open`: not started or still materially incomplete.
- `In progress`: started but not done.
- `Review`: drafted or repaired, needs review.
- `Done`: complete for the named workspace item at the current beta/proof gate.
- `Decision`: needs an explicit author decision before work proceeds.
- `External`: depends on external publication work that cannot be completed
  inside the workspace, with the publication impact named.
- `Deferred`: intentionally not required for this self-published release, with
  the publication impact named.

## 11. Final Acceptance Checklist

The book is done when all workspace-controllable P0 items are closed and every
remaining P0/P1/P2 item is either done, marked `External` with publication
impact named, or marked `Deferred` with a post-launch rationale. External items
must be completed before public launch unless their row explicitly says they
are post-launch work; deferred items are intentionally not required for this
self-published release.

Publication-ready means:

- The final length is intentional.
- Every chapter has a clear claim, concrete scene, control lesson, artifact,
  objection, and bridge.
- The running support-credit workflow is clearly composite unless sourced.
- Major empirical claims are cited, softened, or removed.
- All final citations resolve to verified or archived sources.
- The manuscript uses one citation style consistently.
- Terms do not drift across chapters.
- Beta-reader feedback has been triaged and addressed.
- Ebook and paperback files have passed local export checks, and final
  KDP/device/physical proof inspection is complete.
- Amazon metadata, cover, description, categories, keywords, and author page are
  ready.
- Launch goals and review-copy plan are explicit.
