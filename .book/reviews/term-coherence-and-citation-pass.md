# Term Coherence And Citation Pass

Date: 2026-06-21

Supersession note: this is a historical review record. Current citation style,
citation readiness, and beta/proof package status are governed by
`publication-readiness-tracker.md`, `source-ledger.md`, and
`package-manifest.md`.

Scope: `.book/chapters/*.md`, `.book/source-ledger.md`, and prior review notes.

## Review Standard

This pass used the book's term ledger:

- `draft` and `proposal` before acceptance;
- `evidence` for claim support, not general context;
- `verification` for checking, not permission;
- `approval` only where a named role or policy grants permission;
- `acceptance` for the workflow state that allows use;
- `authority` for the right to affect a decision, record, customer, money, or
  system state;
- `control` only where something can block, route, require, log, throttle,
  escalate, or stop work;
- `trustworthy` as a workflow condition, not a permanent property of a model
  output.

## Findings Addressed

1. Some chapter language used `AI recommendation` where the term was doing
   proposal-state work. Revised those instances to `AI-generated proposal`
   where authority had not been granted.
2. Several sentences used `prove` where the workflow only had support,
   documentation, or establishment of a narrower claim. Revised those to
   `show`, `establish`, `document`, or `support`.
3. The phrase `accepted authority` collapsed two terms. Revised it to
   `accepted work` or `a decision with operational authority`, depending on the
   sentence.
4. The phrase `trustworthy AI systems` assigned trust to the system too broadly.
   Revised it to `AI workflows need records...`, keeping trust attached to the
   workflow conditions.
5. Chapter 5 and Chapter 11 bracket source IDs were converted to author-date
   citations.
6. `source-ledger.md` now includes reviewed rows for A2, A3, A6, A7, A8, A9,
   G1, G2, and G3.

## Remaining Caveats

- Bracket source IDs remain in process documents where they explain draft
  source notation. They no longer remain in chapter prose.
- The source ledger makes the non-case sources citation-ready for draft use,
  but final bibliography style is still open between Chicago author-date and
  notes-bibliography.
- Real-case citations have since been converted from source IDs to author-date
  citations. The case source ledger still keeps `RC*` handles for internal
  routing only.
