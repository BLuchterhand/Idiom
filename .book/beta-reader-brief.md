# Beta Reader Brief

Thank you for reading this draft of **Trust the Process, Not the Prompt**.

This controlled beta is designed for 8-12 early readers. Please return feedback
within 10 business days if possible, so severe factual, evidence, terminology,
or trust-breaking issues can be triaged before public launch.

The book's promise is practical: help a serious team design one AI workflow
where AI output begins as a proposal and becomes accepted work only through
evidence, freshness, permissions, review capacity, accountable acceptance, and
learning.

The running support-credit workflow is a composite design scenario unless a
chapter explicitly names a real case.

Please focus feedback on the questions below.

## Feedback Questions

1. Where did the book earn your trust?
2. Where did it overclaim or need stronger evidence?
3. Where did you get bored or feel the book repeated itself?
4. Which artifact would you actually use on Monday?
5. Which term was unclear or used inconsistently?
6. Which chapter felt most useful?
7. Which chapter could be shorter?
8. What would stop you from recommending this to a leader or senior
   practitioner?

## Feedback Labels

When possible, label comments as:

- factual;
- structural;
- evidence;
- terminology;
- reader-handle;
- style;
- production;
- preference.

The most useful comments name the exact place where trust, clarity, or utility
breaks.
