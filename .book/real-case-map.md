# Real Case Map

Purpose: track the real-world cases used as short evidence callouts in the
chapter drafts. Full citation details live in `source-ledger.md`.

| Chapter | Control Lesson | Case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 00 Opening Teardown | Customer-facing generated guidance needs source authority, review, routing, and failure states before reliance. | Air Canada chatbot / Moffatt v. Air Canada (Moffatt v. Air Canada 2024; Lifshitz and Hung 2024) |
| 01 Category Error | Generated research is a proposal until checked against an authoritative record. | Mata v. Avianca fake citations (Mata v. Avianca 2023) |
| 02 How Important Work Earns Trust | Consequential work needs evidence, independent review authority, acceptance gates, and failure states that can stop release. | Boeing 737 MAX certification (Joint Authorities Technical Review 2019; Federal Aviation Administration 2020; U.S. House Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure 2020) |
| 03 Artifact Fixes Do Not Control The Decision | Disclosure, human-review language, warnings, and corrections help only when publication can reject the artifact first. | CNET AI-written articles (Sato and Roth 2023; Christian 2023) |
| 04 Good AI Task Shape | A support task has to be split into prevention, education, triage, crisis routing, and clinical advice before AI belongs in the path. | NEDA Tessa chatbot (Vinall 2023; Wells 2023; Knight 2023) |
| 05 Evidence And Freshness | Useful context should not become controlling evidence for a claim it cannot establish. | Ofqual 2020 grading algorithm (Ofqual 2020; House of Commons Education Committee 2020; University of Bristol Centre for Multilevel Modelling n.d.; Richardson 2020) |
| 06 Responsibility Split | Accountability without evidence, authority, and protection becomes blame. | Post Office Horizon (Bates and Others v. Post Office Ltd 2019; Post Office Horizon IT Inquiry n.d.) |
| 07 Effect Boundaries | Probabilistic systems need states where action authority is removed, not merely reduced. | Cruise pedestrian-dragging incident (NHTSA 2023; California Department of Motor Vehicles 2023; NHTSA 2024; Associated Press 2023; The Guardian 2023) |
| 08 Accepted Verified Output | Generated or enforced determinations are not accepted verified output without evidence, review, and due process. | Michigan MiDAS unemployment fraud determinations (Cahoo v. SAS Analytics Inc. 2019; Michigan Attorney General 2022; Oosting 2024; University of Michigan Science, Technology, and Public Policy Program 2024) |
| 09 Human Review | Human presence is not oversight without attention, information, authority, and intervention support. | Uber automated-vehicle crash (National Transportation Safety Board 2019) |
| 10 Bounded Agent | Tool-mediated action needs action-specific limits, automated stops, incident authority, and disconnect conditions. | Knight Capital trading incident (Securities and Exchange Commission 2013a; Securities and Exchange Commission 2013b) |
| 11 Evals And Governance | Incident evidence should change operating scope, maps, controls, or release conditions. | Waymo flooded-road recall (NHTSA 2026a; NHTSA 2026b; NHTSA 2026c; NHTSA 2026d) |
| 12 Trustworthy Workflow | Narrow corpus, source links, internal use, feedback, and professional authority form a concrete deployment pattern. | Morgan Stanley internal GPT-4 assistant (Morgan Stanley 2023; OpenAI 2024) |
| 13 Working With The Machine | Professional review does not make probabilistic output authoritative without claim and source verification. | Deloitte Australia government report (McGuirk 2025; Australian Department of Employment and Workplace Relations 2025a; Australian Department of Employment and Workplace Relations 2025b; Australian Department of Employment and Workplace Relations 2026) |
