# External Launch Gates

These items are outside the workspace and must be completed before public
launch. They are marked `External` in `publication-readiness-tracker.md` rather
than treated as done.

## Source And Citation Gates

- Final browser/archive verification for sources that are blocked, paywalled,
  or unstable under command-line checks.
- Archive or replace any final bibliography link that cannot be verified in the
  production workflow.

Launch impact: do not publish publicly until final citations are reachable,
archived, or replaced.

## Beta Reader Gates

- Send the generated EPUB/HTML or clean PDF package to beta readers.
- Collect feedback using `beta-reader-brief.md`.
- Triage severe feedback as factual, structural, evidence, terminology,
  reader-handle, style, production, or preference.
- Resolve severe factual, evidence, or trust-breaking findings before public
  launch.

Launch impact: the workspace package is ready for beta, but public launch waits
for severe external beta findings to be resolved or explicitly accepted.

## KDP And Proof Gates

- Upload the ebook and paperback files to KDP.
- Inspect Kindle/KDP Previewer rendering.
- Use KDP's current category selector and print-cost calculator.
- Record the KDP AI-content disclosure answer from final manuscript and cover
  production records.
- Produce the final paperback wrap after KDP page count/spine dimensions are
  known.
- Order and inspect a physical proof or equivalent KDP proof path.

Launch impact: do not turn on public paperback sales until cover, spine,
tables, citations, links, and appendix formatting have been proofed.

## Review And Advertising Gates

- Collect honest early public reviews after launch.
- Run Amazon Sponsored Products only after the page, sample, reviews, and
  conversion signals are stable.
- Consider BookBub or newsletter promotion only after pricing and review proof
  are stable.

Launch impact: advertising is intentionally deferred until conversion risk is
lower.
