# Book Writing Skill Suite

Purpose: map the reusable Codex writing skills to this manuscript's revision
passes. The skills live in the local Codex skills directory so they can be
reused beyond this book.

## Skills

- `technical-book-architect`: whole-book promise, reader transformation,
  chapter order, argument arc, and source plan.
- `technical-chapter-architect`: chapter packets, chapter boundaries, section
  order, chapter claims, and reusable takeaways.
- `technical-book-flow-editor`: cross-chapter continuity, running example
  re-entry, concept sequencing, transitions, and pacing.
- `technical-book-style-editor`: professional technical tone, sentence clarity,
  paragraph shape, terminology, and line edits.
- `technical-book-open-close`: book introduction, chapter openings, section
  openings, endings, bridges, and closing chapter.
- `technical-case-evidence-editor`: real cases, case placement, evidence
  strength, source caveats, diagrams, quotes, and citations.

## Recommended Revision Passes

1. **Architecture pass:** use `technical-book-architect` against
   `north-star.md`, `argument-map.md`, `outline-v2-compact.md`, and the chapter
   list.
2. **Chapter pass:** use `technical-chapter-architect` on one chapter at a time.
3. **Flow pass:** use `technical-book-flow-editor` across adjacent chapter
   groups: 00-03, 04-07, 08-11, 12-13.
4. **Evidence pass:** use `technical-case-evidence-editor` with
   `source-ledger.md`, `real-case-map.md`, and `real-case-audit.md`.
5. **Opening and closing pass:** use `technical-book-open-close` for chapter
   first pages, final pages, and bridges.
6. **Line edit pass:** use `technical-book-style-editor` after structure is
   stable.

## Book-Specific Rule

Do not polish around a structural weakness. If a chapter lacks a reader
question, real mechanism, control, tradeoff, or usable handle, fix the chapter
architecture before line editing.
