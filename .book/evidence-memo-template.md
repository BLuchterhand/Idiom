## Allowed Verb Strength

Choose one:

- **Observed**: sourced evidence supports "does," "can," or "has happened."
- **Common pattern**: multiple sources support "often" or "in many workflows."
- **Design thesis**: use "should," "treat as," or "a practical standard is."
- **Hypothesis**: use "may," "can create pressure toward," or "should be tested."
